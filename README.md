💰 Simple Money Tracker
A simple and lightweight personal money management app for tracking daily spending in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR).

The goal of this app is to make recording expenses quick and easy, while providing a clear monthly overview of where your money goes.

✨ Features
📝 Add a new spending record

📅 Record the date of each spending

💵 Record the amount in IDR

🏷️ Categorize spending

📊 View monthly spending reports

📋 See spending summaries by category

🔎 Easily review spending history

Spending Categories
Example categories include:

🍜 Food

🛒 Daily

☕ Coffee

🚗 Transport

💡 Utility

🏠 Other

📱 How It Works
Adding an expense should be as simple as possible.

For each spending record, you only need to provide:

Field	Example
Category	Food
Date	27 September 2026
Amount	Rp25,000

After adding the expense, the app stores it and includes it in the monthly report.

📊 Monthly Report
At the end of each month, the app provides a summary of your spending.

Example
September 2026

Total Spending: Rp3,250,000

Category	Amount
🍜 Food	Rp1,200,000
🛒 Daily	Rp500,000
☕ Coffee	Rp350,000
🚗 Transport	Rp450,000
💡 Utility	Rp750,000
Total	Rp3,250,000

This makes it easy to understand:

How much money was spent during the month

Which categories received the most spending

How much was spent on each category

Your overall spending pattern
